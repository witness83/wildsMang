Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Wilds Mang (the "Game"). We respect and protect players' personal information. Please read this policy before using the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information during normal operation.

  • Game saves: coins, unlocked characters, unlocked equipment, achievement progress, and settings (language, sound, haptics). These are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to any server.
  • What we do not collect: name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, call records, or any other sensitive personal data.

2. Local Storage

Saves are written inside the app sandbox on your device. Uninstalling the Game removes them. We cannot access, read, or restore your local saves remotely.

3. Permissions

The Game requests only the permissions needed for features you actively use.

  • Haptic feedback: used for vibration on hits and pickups. It can be disabled in Settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of the Game contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs, and no tracking links to external sites.

App Store downloads, updates, and reviews are provided by Apple and are governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect age or identity information. If you are a guardian and believe we hold information about a child, please contact us below.

6. Data Security

Because the Game does not collect or upload personal information, there is no server-side data breach risk. The security of local saves depends on your device's system settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

If a future version introduces new data practices (for example, online features or in-app purchases), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
